# RoamReady

RoamReady is a travel planner that keeps the practical parts of a trip next to the places you actually want to go. Plan flights, hotels, and the day’s stops in one itinerary, then get suggestions that fit how you like to travel and whether you have a car.

The product is a web app and an [Android app](https://github.com/tdunckel/RoamReady_Android). Both use the same account and the same trips.

## What you can do

- **Start a trip** by talking through where you are going, when, and how you will get around.
- **Build the itinerary** day by day. Reservations such as flights and hotels stay put. Other stops can be reordered, left unplanned, or placed on a day when you are ready.
- **Discover places** along the way. The Digital Sherpa suggests stops, including meals when a day does not already have them, and shows them on a map.
- **Share a trip** with other travelers so you can plan together.
- **Forward confirmation emails** to the trip’s address and turn them into reservations.

Recommendations respect mobility. A hike that is too far to reach without a car is marked as needing transport when you do not have one.

## Who it is for

During sign-up, RoamReady asks how you like to travel:

- **Peak Seeker** — outdoors and big experiences
- **Cultural Curator** — food, arts, and local places
- **Logistics Lead** — smooth routing, reliable lodging, and less friction

That choice shapes what the app suggests. You can still plan any kind of trip.
